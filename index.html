Summer Classes

Engage your child in fun and educational summer experiences with activities that nurture creativity, learning, and social skills. Our summer programs are designed for children from K1 to Grade 5 to ensure a memorable and enriching learning journey.

ROPRISA Talent Show

KiddieLamp proudly participates in the **ROPRISA (Regional Organization of Private Schools)** Talent Show, a vibrant showcase where students from various private schools compete and collaborate.

It’s an incredible opportunity for our young stars to display their talents in singing, dancing, public speaking, and the arts, fostering confidence and healthy competition in a supportive environment. We celebrate every achievement!

Safety & Compliance

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of safety, quality, and government compliance. Our facilities are regularly assessed by DEPED and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) to ensure a secure and ideal learning environment.

DEPED Certified

Fully compliant with the Department of Education's standards for curriculum, facilities, and teaching staff.

BFP Approved

All safety equipment, egress points, and procedures meet strict fire safety regulations. Your child's well-being is our top priority.
